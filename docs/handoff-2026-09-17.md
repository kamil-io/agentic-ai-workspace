## Business direction

Robot People Industries is a solo business that develops agentic systems for companies. Current revenue is about RM300 per month. The owner needs at least RM5,000 per month to work on the business full time. The immediate commercial need is a simple, repeatable service offer and a sales pipeline. Longer-term opportunities include selling the deployed system and teaching courses based on the implementation.

The operating model uses one Hermes runtime with named roles. Ahmad is the main conversational entry point: the owner gives ideas to Ahmad, Ahmad remembers and plans them, then delegates specialist work. Separate containers for every named agent are currently unnecessary and would waste OCI memory.
